Sentiment Analysis Comparison for Decision Support

Problem
Text feedback requires interpretable sentiment methods that account for coverage and context limitations.
Approach
Compared Naive Bayes, logistic regression, VADER, TextBlob, and emoticon matching using 20,000 public tweets; explored a proposed radiology feedback application.
Impact
The full rerun reproduced the 20,770-term vocabulary and found 496 unique emoticon matches with no overlap between categories. Sentence perturbations illustrate score sensitivity; no holdout accuracy or healthcare outcome was evaluated.
